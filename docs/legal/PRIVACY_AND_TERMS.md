# Privacy Policy and User Agreement

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026 · **Version:** 1.0

RAIA (Responsible AI Assistant) is a research prototype developed as part of a
master's research project on Responsible AI. This page explains what RAIA does
with your information and the terms under which you use it. It applies to the
hosted RAIA application and to everyone who signs in to it.

> This document is maintained by the RAIA project and should be reviewed by the
> responsible researcher and the institution before the application is opened to
> participants.

## 1. Who is responsible

RAIA is operated by the RAIA research project. Questions about this policy,
your data or your rights can be sent to the contact below.

**Contact:** {contact}

## 2. Information we collect

**From your Google account.** When you choose *Sign in with Google*, Google
shares with RAIA your **email address**, your **name** and whether Google has
**verified** that email address. RAIA does not receive your password, contacts,
files, calendar or any other Google data, and requests no other permissions.

**Information you create in RAIA.**

- Projects you create or join, and their content: form answers, drafts,
  approved documents, open issues and the project activity log.
- Your role on each project and the invitations you send or receive.
- Your assessment answers, if you take part in the evaluation, including the
  optional profile questions (broad categories only, such as region or years of
  experience).

**Technical information.** The date of your first sign-in, your last sign-in,
when you accepted this agreement and a daily count of agent runs used to
enforce fair-use limits.

## 3. How we use information

- **To provide the service:** to sign you in, show you your projects, and
  control who can see and change each project.
- **To keep an accountable record:** approvals are recorded under your
  signed-in identity, because accountability is the purpose of RAIA's human
  approval gates. Project members can see who approved what.
- **For research:** activity events and assessment answers are analysed to
  evaluate RAIA. Research exports replace your identity with a participant code
  and never include your name or email address. Results are published only in
  aggregate.

RAIA does not sell information, does not use it for advertising and does not
use Google user data to develop or train AI models.

## 4. Sharing

- **Project members** see the content of projects they belong to, including
  the names of the people who approved each stage.
- **Language-model provider.** To draft their analyses, the agents send the
  content of the stage being run (your form answers and the relevant approved
  project documents) to the configured language-model provider. Your Google
  account details are not sent.
- **Infrastructure providers** host the application and its database on
  RAIA's behalf, under their own security commitments.
- **Legal obligations:** information may be disclosed where the law requires it.

## 5. Confidentiality

RAIA treats project content as confidential to the members of that project.
The research team does not read project content other than through the
pseudonymized research export, and does not publish it. Free-text assessment
comments may be quoted in research publications without any identifying
information.

**Your commitment.** Do not enter into RAIA personal data about other people,
trade secrets or information you are not authorized to share. Use fictional or
anonymized scenarios when in doubt; the demo project is provided for that
purpose.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Information is kept while your account exists and for as long as the research
project needs it. You can, at any time, from **Settings**:

- download a copy of the information RAIA holds about you;
- delete projects you own;
- leave projects you were invited to;
- delete your account.

Deleting your account removes your account, memberships, invitations and
assessment answers, and every project of which you are the only owner.
Approvals you recorded on projects owned by other people remain in those
projects' audit trails, since removing them would falsify the record.
Pseudonymized data already included in a research export cannot be traced back
to you and is not deleted.

## 7. Your rights

Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) and
similar laws, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction,
anonymization or deletion of your data, information about sharing, and
withdrawal of consent. Most of these are available directly in Settings; for
anything else, use the contact in section 1.

## 8. Security

Access to every project is checked on each request against your membership.
Sign-in is handled by Google; RAIA stores no passwords. Approved project
documents are kept in a tamper-evident history. No system is perfectly secure;
tell us promptly if you believe your account has been misused.

## 9. User agreement

By signing in you agree that:

1. RAIA is a **research prototype** provided free of charge and "as is". It may
   change, be unavailable or be discontinued.
2. RAIA's outputs are **drafts to support human judgement**, not legal,
   compliance or professional advice. You remain responsible for decisions
   made with them, which is why every stage requires a person's approval.
3. You will use RAIA lawfully, will not attempt to access projects you were not
   invited to, and will not attempt to disrupt the service or misuse the
   agents.
4. You keep ownership of the content you enter. You allow RAIA to store and
   process it as described in this policy.
5. Access can be withdrawn from accounts that break these terms.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above changes and you
will be asked to accept the new version the next time you sign in.
